# Feather Fencer Fechtbuch

A personal, public knowledge base for historical European martial arts (HEMA).

This site is the starting point for a growing collection of research notes,
source-based interpretation, and fencing practice. Research articles will be
added gradually; no chat archive has been imported.

## Explore

- [About this project](about.md): scope and publication principles.
- [Work in progress](wip/index.md): clearly marked unfinished notes.

Use the search field to search the full text of published pages.

## Reading this wiki

Final articles are written in English. Work-in-progress notes may be in Czech.
All material in this repository, including drafts, is public.

Historical sources, interpretations, hypotheses, and modern training models
should be clearly distinguished. An unfinished note is not a settled conclusion.
